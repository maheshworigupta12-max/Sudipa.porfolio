Mitigation of O/W Emulsion Fouling Using PSF Ultrafiltration Membrane Modified with Conjugate Polymer Based Composite
Student Researcher · Oct 2025 – Present
Developing hydrophilic polysulfone (PSF) membranes enhanced with conjugate polymer composites to address oil-fouling challenges in industrial wastewater treatment. The research targets a critical problem in membrane separation technology — oil/water emulsion fouling — which degrades membrane performance and increases operational costs at scale. By engineering surface hydrophilicity through controlled polymer modification, this work seeks to extend membrane lifespan and improve industrial filtration efficiency.
